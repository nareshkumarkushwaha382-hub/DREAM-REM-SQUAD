# Dream Rem Squad (DRS)

> **"DREAM REM SQUAD WAS HERE."**

Dream Rem Squad (DRS) is a structured, long-term international fan community foundation dedicated to Rem and the atmospheric dark-fantasy universe of *Re:ZERO - Starting Life in Another World*. 

Designed from the ground up to transcend temporary fan pages, DRS provides a permanent digital home, rigorous community governance, spoiler-protected archives, and collaborative creative showcases for admirers worldwide.

---

## 1. Mission
**"Build a welcoming worldwide community for people who appreciate Rem and the world of Re:ZERO."**

DRS exists to celebrate Rem’s profound emotional depth, loyalty, and resilience while fostering a supportive, organized, and creative environment for fans across borders.

---

## 2. Vision
Our long-term vision is to evolve into a globally recognized fan organization featuring synchronized international community art showcases, curated lore archives, and collaborative multi-platform chapters that honor the legacy of *Re:ZERO*.

---

## 3. What DRS Is
Dream Rem Squad is an independent, community-driven initiative built by fans, for fans. It functions as an open cultural anchor—providing clean user interfaces, structured knowledge repositories, and strict adherence to creative attribution standards without commercial motives.

---

## 4. Current Development Status
- **Current Version:** v1.0 Foundation Release (Static Architecture)
- **Active Status:** Fully deployed and operational on static hosting environments.
- **Membership Status:** Public archives and galleries are fully open. Backend membership registration systems are currently in active design and development.

---

## 5. Website Pages
The DRS web platform is structured into a cohesive multi-page architecture:
- **`index.html`** – Homepage featuring the atmospheric night-sky hero, core pillars, bulletin updates, and community status placeholders.
- **`about.html`** – Detailed breakdown of DRS mission, philosophy, long-term vision, and participation scope.
- **`join.html`** – Membership explanation, community guidelines preview, privacy standards, and future platform hubs.
- **`archive.html`** – Structured character database with an interactive spoiler-warning protection system.
- **`fan-art.html`** – Community art gallery featuring category filters, live search, artist attribution fields, and a lightbox modal.
- **`wallpapers.html`** – Atmospheric night-sky desktop and mobile wallpaper downloads.
- **`events.html`** – Calendar of upcoming community art challenges and collaborative initiatives.
- **`rules.html`** – Comprehensive 8-point community code of conduct and moderation standards.
- **`history.html`** – Chronological timeline tracking DRS milestones and foundational growth.
- **`community.html`** – Official platform link hub.

---

## 6. Current Features
- **Atmospheric Night-Sky Aesthetics:** Custom CSS variables, subtle animated starry backgrounds, deep sapphire-blue palettes, and careful glassmorphism.
- **Interactive Spoiler Protection System:** Click-to-reveal overlay masks on archive sections to protect newer fans from accidental spoilers across story arcs.
- **Dynamic Fan Art Gallery:** Real-time client-side search and category filtering paired with a high-resolution lightbox preview modal.
- **Responsive Mobile Navigation:** Hamburger menu toggle supporting seamless adaptation across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and widescreen desktop displays.
- **Accessibility & Performance:** Semantic HTML5 markup, keyboard navigation support, high color contrast ratios, and zero external build dependencies.

---

## 7. Planned Features
- **Community Chapters:** Regional localization and localized community coordination hubs.
- **Interactive Lore Extensions:** Expanded multi-arc timelines and character relationship maps.
- **Submissions Portal:** Secure community upload interface for verified artist submissions.
- **Lightweight Membership Badges:** Non-intrusive community identification tokens.

---

## 8. Technology Stack
- **HTML5** (Semantic structure, Open Graph metadata, accessibility attributes)
- **CSS3** (Modern Flexbox/Grid layouts, CSS Custom Properties, smooth transitions)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Zero dependencies, modular UI controllers for modals, filters, and spoilers)
- **Hosting Target:** GitHub Pages (Static deployment)
