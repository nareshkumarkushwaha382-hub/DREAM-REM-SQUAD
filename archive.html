Basic Information

Spoiler-Free

Rem is one of the maid twins working at Roswaal L. Mathers' mansion, alongside her older sister, Ram. Recognizable by her iconic light blue hair covering her right eye and maid uniform, Rem possesses profound dedication and exceptional competence.

Role Maid at Roswaal Mansion
Affiliation Mathers Household
Signature Weapon Morningstar (Flail)

Personality & Demeanor

Spoiler-Free

Initially cautious and polite on the surface, Rem tends to harbor low self-esteem due to past childhood events involving her sister. However, upon forming deep emotional bonds, she exhibits fierce loyalty, culinary mastery, meticulous housekeeping, and unyielding emotional fortitude.

Abilities & Combat (Oni Form)

Spoiler Warning: Season 1
⚠️ Contains Season 1 Spoilers

This section discusses combat capabilities, Oni lineage traits, and magic aptitude. Click to reveal.

As a member of the Oni race, Rem can manifest a single horn on her forehead, which significantly amplifies her physical strength, agility, and absorption of ambient mana.

She excels in water magic—capable of casting powerful offensive ice projectiles, healing spells, and freezing obstacles. Combined with her spiked morningstar, she is a formidable combatant in battle.

Story Development & Later Material

Spoiler Warning: Season 2 & Beyond
⚠️ Contains Major Story Spoilers (Season 2+)

This section discusses major plot developments, memory loss, and subsequent story arcs. Click to reveal.

Following encounters with the Archbishop of Gluttony, Rem's presence and memories are erased from the collective consciousness of those around her, casting her into a deep slumber.

Her awakening and subsequent journey in later arcs mark a profound evolution in the overarching narrative of Re:ZERO, highlighting her enduring spirit across new territories and trials.
