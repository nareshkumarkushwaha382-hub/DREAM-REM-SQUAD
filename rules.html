Governance & Code

DRS Community Rules

To ensure Dream Rem Squad remains a welcoming, professional, and positive worldwide community, all members are expected to follow these fundamental standards.

01

Respect Other Members

Treat everyone with courtesy. Healthy debate is encouraged, but hostility, personal attacks, or condescension will not be tolerated.

02

No Harassment or Bullying

Zero tolerance for harassment, hate speech, stalking, or targeted bullying of any kind against members or creators.

03

Credit Artists & No Plagiarism

Always attribute fan artists accurately. Do not repost artwork without permission or claim credit for creations that are not your own.

04

Proper Spoiler Marking

Protect newer fans by using clear spoiler warnings and tags when discussing advanced light novel or seasonal story material.

05

No Character Harassment

Appreciating Rem does not require tearing down other characters or fans of other Re:ZERO characters. Keep discussions constructive.

06

Protect Personal Privacy

Never share private personal information (doxxing), phone numbers, or sensitive data of fellow community members.

07

No Spam or Self-Promotion Abuse

Keep channels clean. Unsolicited commercial spam or repetitive disruptive advertising is strictly prohibited.

08

Keep Discussions Friendly

Foster an inviting, supportive atmosphere where fans of all backgrounds feel comfortable participating.
