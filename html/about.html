Identity & Vision

About Dream Rem Squad

"DREAM REM SQUAD WAS HERE." — The philosophy behind our international community.

The DRS Mission

"Build a welcoming worldwide community for people who appreciate Rem and the world of Re:ZERO."

Why DRS Was Created

Rem represents unwavering loyalty, profound emotional depth, and inspiring resilience in the face of despair. DRS was founded to give admirers of her character an organized, permanent home where creativity, respectful discussion, and global camaraderie can thrive without clutter or toxicity.

Community Philosophy

We believe fan communities should prioritize inclusivity, artistic attribution, and genuine mutual support. Whether you fell in love with her gentle maid demeanor or her fierce combat strength, every fan has a voice here.

Long-Term Vision

Over the coming years, DRS aims to expand into structured worldwide chapters, high-quality community art anthologies, synchronized global community events, and an interactive sanctuary for Re:ZERO literature and animation enthusiasts.

What Members Can Participate In

🎨 Fan Art Showcases

Submit and discover curated community artwork with standardized artist credits.

🏆 Seasonal Challenges

Take part in community-wide art challenges, wallpaper creation events, and trivia nights.

📚 Spoiler-Safe Archives

Contribute to and browse structured lore databases categorized safely by story arcs.
