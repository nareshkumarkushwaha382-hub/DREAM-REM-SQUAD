What Joining DRS Means

Joining Dream Rem Squad means aligning with a community dedicated to positivity, mutual respect, and deep appreciation for Rem. Whether you participate by browsing our spoiler-safe archives, submitting fan artwork, downloading custom wallpapers, or taking part in community events, your presence contributes to our growing worldwide identity.

🔒 Current Membership Status & Privacy Expectations

DRS membership registration is currently being developed. For version 1, there is no fake account registration system, login portal, or database.

We respect your digital privacy: DRS does not collect passwords, physical addresses, phone numbers, or unnecessary personal information. You can freely explore all public archives, galleries, and community resources without creating an account.
